Install in Chrome in 30 Seconds

No Chrome Web Store account needed. Follow these 3 simple steps:

1

Download & Unzip

Click the download button to get the meetstudio-extension.zip file, then right-click and choose Extract All to any folder.

⬇️ Download meetstudio-extension.zip
2

Open Chrome Extensions

Open a new tab in Google Chrome, copy the address below, and paste it into your address bar:

chrome://extensions
3

Enable & Load Unpacked

In the top-right corner of chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click "Load unpacked" (top-left), and select the extracted folder.

✓ That's it! Open meet.google.com to start recording!
